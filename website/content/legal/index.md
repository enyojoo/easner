# Legal

Easner Group, Inc. (“Easner”) builds financial technology for global money movement. Easner is **not a bank**. Banking, money transmission, payment acceptance, verification, and related regulated services are provided by **licensed partners** under their licenses and terms. Wallet signing for digital asset features is provided by **Turnkey** under the self-custody model described in our Terms and Privacy Policy.

For help, use [support@easner.com](mailto:support@easner.com) or live chat in the app or Business dashboard.
