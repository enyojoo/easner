# Disclosure: Easner Business Banking

Last updated: November 6, 2026

**Easner Business Banking** is the business banking, payouts, payroll, collections, and online payments experience on the Easner web dashboard, offered by **Easner Group, Inc.**

## Easner is not a bank

Easner is a **financial technology company, not a bank**. Easner does not offer FDIC or equivalent deposit insurance and does not itself hold customer deposits.

KYB, virtual accounts, money transmission, corridor payouts, and card acceptance (where enabled) are provided by **licensed partners** under their licenses and terms. The regulated provider for a specific service is identified in hosted onboarding, funding instructions, and transaction records where required.

## Products and verification

Access depends on successful **business verification**, jurisdiction, and enabled features. Typical products include **global banking**, **more accounts**, **online payments** (card acceptance on invoices via Stripe Connect where enabled), **payroll**, **collections** (invoicing, Terminal, QR Pay), and **wallet / stablecoin** features where enabled. Additional verification may be required for certain corridors or for online payments merchant onboarding.

See the [KYC/KYB and AML Policy](/compliance) for business jurisdiction rules.

## Your responsibilities

You are responsible for authorized users on your account, accurate ownership and KYB information, lawful use of payroll and collections, tax and employment compliance for payroll, and card-network and processor rules for online payments.

Business **Ease** plans (Ease Plus, Ease Pro, and related) are described in Settings → Billing and in the [Terms of Service](/terms). Easner Checkout powers hosted checkout for your customers and plan billing for your business where enabled.
