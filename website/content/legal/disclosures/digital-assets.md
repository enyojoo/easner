# Disclosure: Digital assets and wallet

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Where enabled, Easner may offer stablecoin capabilities (for example **USDC** and **EURC** on supported networks), deposit addresses, wallet-initiated sends, and related balance visibility in **Easner Personal Banking** or **Easner Business Banking**.

## Not bank deposits

Digital assets are **not bank deposits** and are **not FDIC insured**. The regulatory treatment of digital assets varies by jurisdiction. Blockchain transactions may be **public** and **irreversible**. You are responsible for correct wallet addresses, memos, and recipient details. Errors may result in permanent loss of funds.

## Turnkey and self-custody

For Turnkey-backed wallet features, **Easner does not sit in the flow of funds** as a custodian or signing party:

- Easner does not sign or co-sign user transactions.
- Easner does not hold, custody, or export users’ private keys or seed phrases.
- Wallet keys and signing are managed within **Turnkey Global Inc. (Turnkey)** infrastructure.
- Easner controls **product access** (who can initiate actions in our UI or API), not transaction signing after submission.

Product access blocks (verification, security, or compliance) are not the ability to alter, cancel, or reverse a transaction once it has been signed and submitted on-chain.

Fiat money-transmission and virtual-account rails remain separate from this wallet model and are provided by licensed partners under their terms.

## Cross-chain routing

Certain wallet sends may use a **cross-chain routing or bridging provider** where enabled. Easner does not control public blockchains.
