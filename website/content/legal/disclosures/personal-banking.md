# Disclosure: Easner Personal Banking

Last updated: November 6, 2026

**Easner Personal Banking** is the consumer banking and payments experience in the Easner mobile app, offered by **Easner Group, Inc.**

## Easner is not a bank

Easner is a **financial technology company, not a bank**. Easner does not offer FDIC or equivalent deposit insurance and does not itself hold customer deposits.

Identity verification, virtual accounts, fiat pay-in and pay-out, money transmission, and card acceptance (where enabled) are provided by **licensed partners** under their licenses and terms. The regulated provider for a specific service is identified in hosted onboarding, funding instructions, and transaction records where required.

## Products and verification

Access depends on successful **verification**, your jurisdiction, and product availability — not on numbered “tiers.” Typical products include **global banking** (accounts, send and receive, corridors where enabled), **more accounts** (additional currencies where offered), **cards** (when launched), and **wallet / stablecoin** features where enabled. Additional verification steps may be required for certain destinations or products.

See the [KYC/KYB and AML Policy](/compliance) for residence rules.

## Digital assets

Digital asset and stablecoin features are **not bank deposits** and are not FDIC insured. Wallet keys and signing for Turnkey-backed features are managed in **Turnkey’s** infrastructure; Easner is not a custodian or signing party. See [Digital assets and wallet](/legal/disclosures/digital-assets).

## Membership

Paid plans (for example **Ease Plus**) are optional subscriptions billed through Easner Checkout. Plan fees, benefits, and cancellation are described in the [Terms of Service](/terms) and in the app under More → Membership.
